Epoch selection evidence — E0 baseline
======================================

Source: Ultralytics EarlyStopping output at end of training.

Verbatim excerpt:
  "EarlyStopping: Training stopped early as no improvement observed in last
   20 epochs. Best results observed at epoch 64, best model saved as best.pt."

CSV evidence (results.csv; epoch column is 1-indexed as confirmed by the
first data row = 1 and last data row = 84):
  epoch 63 (1-indexed): mAP50-95 = 0.83498
  epoch 64 (1-indexed): mAP50-95 = 0.87789   ← tied for max
  epoch 65 (1-indexed): mAP50-95 = 0.87789   ← tied for max

Both epoch 64 and epoch 65 have identical mAP50-95 (0.87789) at recorded
precision. Genuine tie, not a unique argmax. Ultralytics selected epoch 64
per its EarlyStopping message; its internal tie-break logic is not publicly
documented (combined fitness likely includes box loss; first-encountered
max preserved).

Artifact field selected_epoch=64 reflects Ultralytics's choice. The CSV
indexing is 1-indexed: epoch 1 is the first training epoch, epoch 84 is
the last. This matches the Ultralytics message's convention.

Provenance limitation: no per-epoch saved checkpoints (training did not use
save_period=1). Cannot independently diff epoch-64 weights vs epoch-65
weights vs best.pt. Recommend save_period=1 for future runs if strict
verification is required.

Disclosed explicitly; no reconstruction or invented evidence.
